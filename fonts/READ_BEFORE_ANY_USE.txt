First Encounter is a fine signature handwriting script — the kind of cursive you'd recognise as a real person's hand on a card or a contract.

Thin, even pen-pressure throughout, with natural connecting strokes between letters and a small upward bounce in the baseline that keeps a single line from feeling rigid. Capitals carry confident loops; lowercase descenders extend just enough to give phrases air. The italic carries a slightly stronger forward lean.

Picture it as the inked sign-off at the bottom of a letterpress invitation, or running across a small velvet jewellery hangtag.

The font is built with advanced OpenType functionality and guaranteed top-notch quality, containing stylistic and contextual alternates, ligatures and more automatic and manual features; all to give you full control and customizability.
It has extensive lingual support, covering all Latin-based languages, from North Europa to South Africa, from America to South-East Asia. It contains all characters and symbols you'll ever need, including all punctuation and numbers.
